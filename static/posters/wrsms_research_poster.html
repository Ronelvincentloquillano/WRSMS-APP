Objectives of the Study
🔐

General objective. Design, develop, and deploy SmartDynamic Refilling as a secure, scalable, mobile-responsive PWA for real-time and offline management of sales, products, expenses, and forecasts.

🗄️

Data layer. Develop secure storage and retrieval using Python, Django, and PostgreSQL; core modules for sales, orders, inventory/container tracking, and expenses with validation and consistent identifiers.

📈

Analytics & continuity. Develop forecasting and analytics from historical data; implement PWA offline use and sync with deduplication safeguards.

☁️

Deployment. SaaS-style hosting on Render with GitHub version control and auto-deployment for maintainable, collaborative delivery.

Results

WRSMS-PWA was implemented for Elle’s Water Refilling Station: role-based dashboards (Owner/Admin, Staff, Delivery), sales and payments (including offline queue + sync with offline_request_id idempotency), inventory and container tracking, expenses, customer and delivery workflows, reports, basic demand forecasting, and subscription-based access control. The stack matches the manuscript: Django, PostgreSQL, HTML5 / Tailwind CSS / JavaScript, HTTPS, and Render hosting with GitHub auto-deploy. Validation followed the documented strategy: unit, integration, and system testing, plus structured evaluation with station stakeholders (including questionnaires aligned with TAM).

Methodology

Hybrid, iterative approach guided by the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC): requirements (interviews, observation, literature) → design (DFD, ERD, architecture) → iterative build/test → cloud deployment → refinement after user feedback.

Requirements→ Design→ Implement→ Test→ Deploy→ Maintain→ Requirements

Major modules (sales, orders, inventory/containers, expenses, reporting, forecasting, PWA/offline) were refined across iterations. Ethical safeguards followed RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act); production use enforces authentication, RBAC, and encrypted transmission.

Introduction

Philippine water refilling SMEs often depend on manual processes, causing delays, errors, and weak visibility over stock, sales, and expenses. SmartDynamic Refilling addresses this through a relational, web-based platform for inventory, sales, expenses, and reporting, with a mobile-responsive UI. The updated system adds PWA capabilities—offline access, queued transactions with sync on reconnect, visibility of pending records, and safeguards against duplicate posting. While the architecture supports multi-station SaaS growth, this study centers on first practical deployment at Elle’s Water Refilling Station, demonstrating centralized digital workflows, monitoring, and decision-ready outputs for the sector.
